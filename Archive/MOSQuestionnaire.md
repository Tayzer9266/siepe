# MOS Operations Manager Interview Questionnaire
## Pain Points & Improvement Opportunities Discovery

**Purpose:** Gather detailed insights into current operational challenges and validate improvement opportunities  
**Audience:** MOS Operations Manager, Senior Leadership  
**Prepared by:** MOS Transformation Initiative Team  
**Date:** July 1, 2026  
**Estimated Time:** 90-120 minutes (can be split into 2 sessions)

---

## Interview Instructions

### Before the Meeting
- Share these questions in advance so the manager can prepare
- Request 90-120 minutes (or split into 2 sessions covering different topics)
- Ask them to bring specific examples, metrics, or data if available
- Let them know the goal is to understand pain points, not to evaluate performance

### During the Meeting
- Use "tell me more" and "walk me through" to get stories, not just yes/no answers
- Ask for quantification: "How often?" "How long?" "How many?"
- Note pain points with emotional language ("frustrating", "painful", "keeps me up at night")
- Listen for workarounds - they reveal unmet needs
- Capture direct quotes for use in the business case
- Don't defend current systems - focus on understanding their experience

### After the Meeting
- Validate metrics (offer to analyze logs if they don't know exact numbers)
- Shadow the team for a day to see reality vs. description
- Interview 1-2 analysts for front-line perspective
- Talk to a portfolio manager or fund accountant (user perspective)

## 🎯 Section 1: Strategic & Business Impact Questions

### Understanding Business Context

**1. What are the top 3 business priorities for MOS Operations in the next 12 months?**
- _Purpose: Understand strategic alignment and where improvements should focus_
- Follow-up: How does the team's current workload support or hinder these priorities?

**2. How does MOS Operations' performance impact client satisfaction and retention?**
- _Purpose: Quantify business impact of operational issues_
- Follow-up: Can you share a recent example where operations directly impacted a client relationship?

**3. What regulatory or compliance challenges keep you up at night regarding MOS operations?**
- _Purpose: Understand risk and compliance drivers_
- Follow-up: Have auditors raised concerns about any of our processes?

**4. If MOS Operations could do one thing better tomorrow, what would have the biggest business impact?**
- _Purpose: Identify the single highest-value improvement_
- Follow-up: What's preventing you from doing that today?

**5. What metrics does executive leadership care most about for MOS Operations?**
- _Purpose: Understand how success is measured at the top_
- Follow-up: How often do you report these metrics? How long does it take to compile them?

### Risk & Impact

**6. What's the worst operational incident you've experienced in the last year? What was the root cause?**
- _Purpose: Understand severity and frequency of critical issues_
- Follow-up: How long did it take to resolve? What was the business impact?

**7. How many times per month do we delay client reporting due to MOS issues?**
- _Purpose: Quantify client-facing impact_
- Follow-up: What's the typical delay? Hours? Days?

**8. What's the financial exposure when positions don't reconcile or cash doesn't match?**
- _Purpose: Understand dollar risk_
- Follow-up: What's the largest unreconciled amount you've dealt with?

**9. Have we ever had an audit finding related to MOS operations? What was it?**
- _Purpose: Understand compliance risk_
- Follow-up: What process changes did we have to make? Are they sustainable?

**10. What keeps clients from escalating issues - and what pushes them over that line?**
- _Purpose: Understand client tolerance and pain points_
- Follow-up: How many client escalations per quarter?

## ⏱️ Section 3: Daily Operations & Efficiency Questions

### Issue Detection & Response

**19. Walk me through how you discover there's a problem today. What's the typical path?**
- _Purpose: Understand current detection methods_
- Follow-up: How reliable is this method? What do you miss?

**20. How long does it typically take from "issue occurs" to "we know about it"?**

- Follow-up: What's the range? Best case vs. worst case?

**21. What percentage of issues do we catch proactively vs. reactively (client calls)?**
- _Purpose: Understand proactive vs. reactive posture_
- Follow-up: Which issues tend to be discovered by clients?

**22. On average, how many SQL queries do team members run per day? Why?**
- _Purpose: Quantify manual investigation effort_
- Follow-up: How long does the average query investigation take?

**23. What tools or systems do you check first thing every morning? How long does that take?**
- _Purpose: Understand morning routine and manual checking_
- Follow-up: Could any of this be automated?

### Resolution Process

**24. What's the most time-consuming type of issue to resolve? Why?**
- _Purpose: Identify highest-value automation targets_
- Follow-up: How often does this type of issue occur?

**25. Which issues repeat most frequently (daily or weekly)?**
- _Purpose: Identify patterns and automation opportunities_
- Follow-up: Do you resolve them the same way each time?

**26. How often do you need to coordinate with other teams (Asset Admin, Data, IT) to resolve an issue?**
- _Purpose: Understand cross-team dependencies_
- Follow-up: What slows down that coordination?

**27. What percentage of issues could a well-trained analyst resolve independently?**
- _Purpose: Understand skill requirements and empowerment opportunities_
- Follow-up: What holds them back from resolving more independently?

**28. When you resolve an issue, is there a standard process/runbook, or is it figured out each time?**
- _Purpose: Understand process documentation maturity_
- Follow-up: Where are runbooks stored? How often are they used?

### Workload & Volume

**29. What does your team's typical day look like? Walk me through 8am to 5pm.**
- _Purpose: Understand actual daily workflow_
- Follow-up: How much of this is planned vs. interrupt-driven?

**30. What are the peak stress times (time of day, time of month)?**
- _Purpose: Identify capacity planning needs_
- Follow-up: What causes these peaks?

**31. How many issues does the team handle per day on average? Per month-end?**
- _Purpose: Quantify volume_
- Follow-up: Is this growing? At what rate?

**32. What's growing faster: transaction volume, complexity, or support team capacity?**
- _Purpose: Understand sustainability of current model_
- Follow-up: When do you hit a breaking point?
